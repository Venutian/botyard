A LONG RUN CHECKPOINTS
Your run is stopped at its time limit, and what is only in your head is lost. When your run may last over 45 minutes, write one CHECKPOINT line to your ledger (the file your own rules name) every 20 minutes and at the end of each part of the job: the time, what is done, what is next, in one line. At the start of a run, read the newest CHECKPOINT first: when it belongs to the job you were given and is not marked finished, carry on from it instead of starting over. The runner tells you when you are at 80% of your limit: write your checkpoint at once, finish the step you are on, and start nothing large.
